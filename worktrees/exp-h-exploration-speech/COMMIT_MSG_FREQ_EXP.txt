Experiment: Frequency Isolation (Bin 50-60)

Context:
Previous experiment (Clean Physics + Input Norm) achieved 50.8% Global Accuracy across all frequencies.
Hypothesis:
Signal dynamics (phase rotation speed per lag) are frequency dependent. A model trained on ALL frequencies must learn a "compromise" policy or struggle with conflicting patterns.
Isolating a specific band (e.g., Speech formants ~1.5kHz) might yield much higher performance.

Experiment:
- Method: Trained `SeqDT_RTG` on filtering data to only include Frequency Bins 50 to 60 (approx 1500-1800Hz).
- Dataset size: 5410 sequences (vs ~550k global).
- Hyperparameters: Same (epochs=20, lr=5e-4), smaller batch size (64).

Results:
- Global Model Acc: 50.83%
- Filtered (Bin 50-60) Acc: 73.75% (+22.9%)

Conclusion:
1. Significant Performance Gain (+23%): The model performs drastically better when specialized to a narrow frequency band.
2. Interpretation: "General Policy" is hard. The relationship between "Correlation Vector Shape" and "Optimal Lag" varies significantly across frequencies (likely due to wavelength/lag ratio).
3. Strategy Update: Instead of a single shared model, we should consider:
   - Frequency-Conditional Model (Add Freq Embedding).
   - Or Independent Models for sub-bands.
   - Or just verify if "Speech Relevant Bands" (e.g. 50-100) are all learnable together.

This confirms the user's intuition: "Simplifying to fewer/fixed bins increases performance."
