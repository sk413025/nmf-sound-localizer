# Task Packet: Manuscript Revision

## Objective

Revise a manuscript section while preserving or improving claim/evidence integrity.

## Source-of-truth files

- `paper/manuscript/manuscript.md`
- `docs/governance/manuscript-contract.md`
- `docs/nature-communications/nature-communications-submission-requirements.md`

## Required skill

- `manuscript-revision`

## Role

- `manuscript-reviser`

## Non-goals

- inventing new evidence
- changing scientific claims without escalation

## Required outputs

- revised text
- short rationale
- any claim-sensitive issues for review

## Acceptance criteria

- section remains manuscript-contract compliant
- evidence anchors remain present
- no unsupported claim expansion

## Escalation conditions

- evidence no longer supports the wording
- the revision materially changes interpretation

## Handoff target

- `supervisor` or `red-team-reviewer`, depending on risk
