# Manuscript Revision

Use this skill for:

- revising Abstract, Introduction, Results, Discussion, or Methods
- tightening claims without losing evidence support
- improving paragraph cohesion, signposting, and manuscript readability

## Required first step

Open and follow:

- `START_HERE_AGENT.md`
- `docs/governance/manuscript-contract.md`
- `docs/agent-ops/task-packets/manuscript-revision-packet.md`

## Workflow

1. Identify the section and its claim burden.
2. Check the relevant figure and Methods anchors before changing wording.
3. Revise for clarity, evidence fit, and manuscript style.
4. Escalate if the revision would alter scientific meaning.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent evidence.
- Do not change claims silently.
- Treat code as substrate; only inspect it when needed to support manuscript fidelity.
