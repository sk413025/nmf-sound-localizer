# Claim Evidence Audit

Use this skill for:

- auditing Results claims
- checking figure-to-claim support
- checking whether narrative statements outrun the available evidence

## Required first step

Open and follow:

- `START_HERE_AGENT.md`
- `docs/governance/manuscript-contract.md`
- `docs/agent-ops/task-packets/claim-audit-packet.md`

## Workflow

1. Identify the target claim.
2. Locate supporting figures, citations, methods, and artifacts.
3. Classify the claim as supported, weak, or unsupported.
4. Hand off concrete fixes to the next role.

## Guardrails

- Audit before rewriting.
- Do not fill evidence gaps with interpretation alone.
- Use evidence paths, not vague critique.
