Directory Structure
-------------------
All datasets follow the structure:
  <dataset_root>/
    angle_0/
      box_000_000.npy (or original_clip_000.npy)
      box_000_001.npy
      box_000_002.npy
    angle_5/
      ...
    [... 35 more angle directories ...]
    angle_180/
      ...

Verification
------------
To verify dataset integrity, run:
  find <dataset_root> -name "*.npy" | wc -l
Expected: 111 files per dataset

To compute MD5 fingerprint:
  find <dataset_root> -name "*.npy" -exec md5sum {} \; | sort | md5sum
