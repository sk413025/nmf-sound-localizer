## Results

### Energy Reduction Comparison

**Definition (used by the validation scripts):**

`Energy reduction = 1 - (||r||² / ||y||²)`,

where `y` is the target LDV STFT window and `r` is the residual after `K=3` selected lags (summed over STFT bins 5–300).

**Evaluation protocol for the reported numbers (reproducible):**
- `clip_idx=0`, `start_t=32`, `Tw=16`, `MaxLag=16`, `K_steps=3`, STFT bins 5–300.
- Oracle computed by `scripts/h_exploration/calc_global_omp.py`.
- DT computed by `scripts/h_exploration/validate_global_dt.py` with `results/dt_freq_aware_full/dt_freq_aware_best.pth`.

| Pursuit Oracle | 97.88% | `python -m scripts.h_exploration.calc_global_omp --clip_idx 0` |

## Physical Interpretation

### What Stage 1 Learned

The Frequency-Aware DTMin learned to select sparse **STFT-frame lags** for Mic→LDV reconstruction **conditioned on the frequency bin**:

```
For each frequency f:
  Input: Mic spectrogram correlation state
  Output: Lag sequence [ℓ₁, ℓ₂, ℓ₃]

Physical meaning:
  - The best discrete lag choices can differ across STFT bins because the complex phase response depends on frequency.
  - For a pure delay τ: H(f) = exp(-j·2π·f·τ), so φ(f) = -2π·f·τ (mod 2π).
  - Conditioning on the frequency bin helps the policy disambiguate phase-wrapped behavior across bins.
```

### Why This Works for Stage 2

The learned features are:
- **Angle-agnostic**: Trained at single angle (90°)
- **Frequency-aware**: Each frequency bin handled appropriately
- **Transferable**: Can be combined with the pre-computed calibration library H_cal(f,θ)
